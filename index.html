About

Music from the hills, kept alive

Pahadi folk music carries the language, seasons and memory of the Himalayas. Much of it lives on cassettes, at village gatherings and in the memory of the people who sing it. HimRaag exists to bring that music somewhere it can be found, played and passed on.

🏔️

Rooted in the region

A catalogue built around Himachali, Garhwali, Kumaoni and wider Himalayan folk traditions — organised by region, language and artist.

🎧

Built for listening

Background playback, lock-screen and Bluetooth controls, offline downloads and a player that stays out of the way of the music.

🤝

Fair to artists

Music and artwork remain the property of the artists and rights-holders. Takedown requests are honoured: rights-holders can ask for any track to be removed through the contact page.

Copyright

Respecting the artists

Ownership stays with the artists

All music, recordings, artwork and metadata in HimRaag remain the property of their respective artists, composers and rights-holders. HimRaag does not claim ownership of any work in its catalogue.

If you are an artist or rights-holder and believe your work appears in HimRaag without permission, we will act on it. Send a notice to sc5271027@gmail.com identifying the work, where it appears in the app, and proof of your ownership or authority to act. Substantiated claims are removed promptly — see the DMCA and copyright section for exactly what to include.

The HimRaag name, logo and application itself are the property of the developer.

Questions about your data?

Privacy questions, deletion requests, bug reports and copyright notices all reach a real inbox. We aim to respond within 2–3 business days.
