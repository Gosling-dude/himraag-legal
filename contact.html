What to write about

One inbox handles everything below. Putting the right words in the subject line helps us reply faster.

Bug reports

Found something broken — playback stopping, a song that will not download, artwork not loading, a crash? Please tell us. Use the subject line “Bug report” and include as much of this as you can:

  • what you were doing when it happened;
  • what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • the song, album or screen involved;
  • your phone model and Android version;
  • whether it happens every time or only sometimes;
  • a screenshot or screen recording, if you have one.

Copyright reports and DMCA requests

HimRaag exists to support Himalayan music and the people who make it. If your work appears in the app without your permission, we will act on it — you do not need a lawyer to write to us.

Send takedown notices to sc5271027@gmail.com

Use the subject line “Copyright notice” or “DMCA”. We review every notice and remove substantiated claims promptly.

Please include all of the following, so we can act without a delay:

  1. Identification of the work — the song, recording, artwork or other material you own.
  2. Where it appears in HimRaag — the exact track, album or artist page.
  3. Proof of ownership or authority — evidence that you are the rights-holder, or are authorised to act for them.
  4. Your contact details — name, email address, and postal address if you are filing a formal DMCA notice.
  5. A good-faith statement — that you believe the use is not authorised by the rights-holder, their agent, or the law.
  6. A statement of accuracy — that the information in your notice is accurate, and that you are the owner or authorised to act on their behalf.
  7. Your signature — physical or electronic.

Counter-notices. If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake or that you hold the rights, write to the same address with an explanation and your evidence. We will review it and restore the content if the claim does not hold up.

Privacy and data requests

To access, correct, export or delete your personal data, email us with the subject line “Data request”. Send it from the address associated with your account so we can verify it is you. We respond within 30 days.

To delete your account specifically, follow the steps on the account deletion page. Your full rights are set out in the Privacy Policy.

Artists and contributors

If you are a Pahadi or Himalayan artist and would like your music on HimRaag — or would like a recording removed — get in touch. Submissions are reviewed before publication, and you can withdraw your work at any time. See section 7 of the Terms.
