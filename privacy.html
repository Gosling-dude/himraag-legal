Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026  ·  Applies to: HimRaag Android app (com.sumitchauhan.himraag)

The short version

HimRaag needs a Google sign-in. We do not sell your data, we show no ads, we do not use the Advertising ID, and we never ask for your location, contacts or microphone. Your favourites, listening history, play history and downloads stay on your phone. Our servers hold your sign-in account, a profile photo if you add one, a daily summary of your app use, and any song requests you send us — and you can delete your account from inside the app.

1. Who we are

HimRaag is developed and operated by Sumit Chauhan, an independent developer based in India.

Contact for privacy questions and data requests: sc5271027@gmail.com

2. Signing in is required

HimRaag has no guest mode. You need to sign in with a Google account before you can browse or listen. Earlier versions offered a guest mode; it has been removed.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Account information

HimRaag uses Firebase Authentication with Google Sign-In. When you sign in with Google, we receive and store:

Account data collected at sign-in and its purpose
DataPurpose
Email addressIdentifying your account
Display nameShowing who is signed in
Profile photo URLShowing your avatar
Google account identifier (UID)Linking your data to your account
Sign-in methodSupporting the correct login flow

We request only basic profile and email scope. We never receive your Google password. Authentication is handled entirely by Google.

Profile photo (optional). If you add a photo in Profile, the picture you choose (made smaller, at most 512 pixels) is stored with your account in Cloud Firestore and shown to you in the app. You can change or delete it at any time, and deleting your account deletes it. You choose the picture with Android’s photo picker or take it with the camera app, so HimRaag needs no photo or camera permission.

3.2 Your library and listening activity (kept on your phone)

The following are stored only on your device, in the app’s local database. They are not uploaded to our servers and do not follow you to another phone:

  • Favourites — songs you have saved
  • Listening history — recently played tracks
  • Settings — for example theme and autoplay
  • Download records — which tracks you saved for offline listening
  • Play history — how often and when you played each song, used for Pahadi Mix, mood playlists and Pahadi Radio
  • Your own background video — if you pick a video to play behind the player, it is copied into the app’s private storage and never uploaded

Signing out leaves this data on the phone. You can clear your listening history in Profile → Clear listening history.

3.3 Play counts

When you have listened to a song for about 30 seconds (or half of a short song), the app counts one play. It keeps these counts on your phone and periodically sends them, with your sign-in, to our server so that each song’s overall play count can be updated. The server is designed to keep only a daily total per account (to limit abuse) and to add your plays to each song’s anonymous total. It does not keep a list of which songs you played.

Activity summary. Since app version 1.0.3, the server also keeps one record per account with your email address, name, when you first and last used the app, and how many songs you played each day. Since version 1.0.4 it also notes which app version you last opened. The developer uses it to see who is testing, who still needs to update, and how much the app is used. It does not say which songs you played, and deleting your account deletes it. Previews in Discover are not counted as plays.

3.4 Song requests

If you use Request a song, we store the details you type (song title, and optionally the singer, a link and notes), what you had typed in Search, and your user ID, name and email address in Cloud Firestore, so that we can find the song and you can see your requests under Profile. We also send a copy of the request, including your name and email address, to the developer as a Telegram message and an email (delivered by Resend), through a small Cloudflare Worker. Song requests are never shown to other listeners.

“Notify me when it’s added”. If you turn this on when you send a request, we save a notification token for your device (from Firebase Cloud Messaging) with that request, and use it only to tell you when that song is on HimRaag. The token is deleted once that notification is sent, and your requests (with any token) are deleted when you delete your account. If you leave it off, no token is saved.

3.5 Recommendations

Recommendations, Pahadi Mix playlists, mood playlists and Pahadi Radio are worked out on your phone from your favourites, your downloads, what you have recently played and your play history. To do this the app looks up those songs in our catalogue. This processing only orders the content shown inside the app. We do not use your data to target advertising, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers for any purpose.

3.6 Downloaded songs

Downloaded tracks are saved to private storage inside the app’s own folder on your device. They are not readable by other apps, are not uploaded anywhere, and are deleted when you remove the download or uninstall the app. The record of which tracks you downloaded is also kept only on your phone, so the app can manage your offline library.

3.7 Analytics and crash reporting

The app includes Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.

Firebase Analytics automatically collects a standard set of app-usage data: app opens, screen views, session length, device model, operating system version, language, an app-instance identifier, and an approximate location derived from your IP address (country/region level, not precise location). In addition, the app records one event of its own, song_play, containing the ID of the song, each time a play is counted (see 3.3). We do not attach your name, email or account ID to Analytics.

The app does not collect the Android Advertising ID. Advertising ID collection is switched off in the app’s configuration and the Advertising ID permission is removed, so Analytics cannot read it.

Firebase Crashlytics collects diagnostic data only when the app crashes: stack traces, the device model and OS version, and app state at the moment of the crash. This is used solely to find and fix defects.

Future analytics

If we add further analytics events to understand which features are used, they will measure app usage only — never the content of your personal communications, and never for advertising. This policy and our Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before any such change ships.

What we never collect

We do not collect precise GPS location. We do not access your contacts, microphone, camera, photos, SMS or call logs. The app requests no such permissions.

3.8 Content delivery

Audio files and artwork are served from Cloudflare (R2 storage, delivered through a Cloudflare Worker). When your device requests a track or an image, Cloudflare receives your IP address and standard request headers, as is unavoidable for any internet content delivery. Cloudflare processes this to deliver content and to protect against abuse.

When the app streams or downloads audio through our content-delivery address (cdn.himraag.in), it adds your Firebase sign-in token to the request so that only signed-in listeners can play the music. That token identifies your account (your user ID, and the name and email address from your Google profile). Our delivery code checks the token and does not store it. Artwork requests carry no sign-in token.

5. Cookies and local storage

The HimRaag Android app does not use cookies. It is a native application with no embedded browser or web view, so no HTTP cookies are ever set or read on your device. This website does not set cookies either — it is static and carries no trackers or analytics.

Instead, the app stores data locally using standard platform mechanisms:

If HimRaag ever offers a web player, cookies or equivalent browser storage may become necessary for sign-in and preferences. Should that happen, we will publish a full cookie notice and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before any non-essential cookie is set.

6. Legal bases for processing (UK/EU users)

  • Contract — account data, play counts and song requests, needed to provide the service you asked for.
  • Legitimate interests — crash reporting and analytics, to keep the app stable and understand broad usage.
  • Consent — where required by local law for analytics identifiers; you may withdraw it by uninstalling the app or contacting us.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Data is processed by these service providers only:

8. Data retention

Account data is kept for as long as your account exists. Song requests are kept until you delete your account or we remove them; a request’s notification token is deleted once its notification is sent. When you delete your account, your sign-in account, your song requests, your profile photo and your activity summary are removed from our database. Copies of song-request alerts already delivered to the developer by Telegram and email stay in the developer’s chat and mailbox until the developer deletes them. Crash and analytics records are retained according to Google Firebase’s standard retention periods. Data stored on your phone (favourites, history, play history, settings, downloads, your own background video, caches) is removed when you uninstall the app.

9. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of your personal data by writing to sc5271027@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to object to processing, to restrict processing, to withdraw consent, or to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Account deletion

You can have your account and its associated data deleted at any time. The full process, including exactly what is removed and what may remain, is set out on our dedicated page:

How to delete your account

In short: open Profile → Delete account in the app. Or email sc5271027@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and we will process the deletion within 30 days. To remove all locally held data immediately, uninstall the app.

11. Children’s privacy

HimRaag is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it. Where local law sets a higher age of digital consent, that higher age applies.

12. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS; the app forbids cleartext traffic at the platform level. Access to our database is restricted by server-side security rules that limit each signed-in user to their own records. Administrative access requires a separately granted privileged credential.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify affected users and the relevant authority where the law requires it.

13. International transfers

Our providers (Google, Cloudflare, Telegram, Resend) operate globally, so your data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. These providers maintain their own safeguards for international transfers.

14. Copyright and DMCA

HimRaag respects the rights of artists and copyright owners. All music, artwork and recordings remain the property of their respective rights-holders.

If you believe content in HimRaag infringes your copyright, send a notice to sc5271027@gmail.com including identification of the work, where it appears in the app, proof of your ownership or authority to act, your contact details, and a statement made in good faith. Substantiated claims are removed promptly. Full details are on the contact page.
