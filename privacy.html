Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026  ·  Applies to: HimRaag Android app (com.sumitchauhan.himraag)

The short version

You can use HimRaag without an account. We do not sell your data, we show no ads, and we never ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. If you sign in, we store your profile and your library so it follows you between devices — and you can have all of it deleted on request.

1. Who we are

HimRaag is developed and operated by Sumit Chauhan, an independent developer based in India.

Contact for privacy questions and data requests: sc5271027@gmail.com

2. Using HimRaag without an account

You can browse and listen as a guest. In guest mode your preferences, favourites and listening history are stored only on your device and are never written to our servers. If you later sign in, the preferences held on the device may be merged into your account.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Account information (only if you sign in)

HimRaag uses Firebase Authentication with Google Sign-In. When you sign in with Google, we receive and store:

Account data collected at sign-in and its purpose
DataPurpose
Email addressIdentifying your account
Display nameShowing who is signed in
Profile photo URLShowing your avatar
Google account identifier (UID)Linking your data to your account
Sign-in methodSupporting the correct login flow
Account creation and last-seen timestampsAccount maintenance

We request only basic profile and email scope. We never receive your Google password. Authentication is handled entirely by Google.

3.2 Your music preferences and activity

Stored in Cloud Firestore against your account so your library follows you between devices:

  • Profile personalization — preferred regions, languages and genres
  • Artists you follow
  • Favourites — songs you have saved
  • Playlists you create
  • Listening history — recently played tracks
  • “Continue listening” position (track and playback position)
  • Total play count

3.3 Recommendations

Personalized recommendations are generated from the preferences and activity listed above — the regions, languages, genres and artists you choose, and what you have listened to. This processing happens to order the content shown inside the app. We do not use your data to target advertising, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers for any purpose.

3.4 Downloaded songs

Downloaded tracks are saved to private storage inside the app’s own folder on your device. They are not readable by other apps, are not uploaded anywhere, and are deleted when you remove the download or uninstall the app. We store only the record of which tracks you chose to download, so the app can manage your offline library.

3.5 Analytics and crash reporting

The app includes Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.

Firebase Analytics automatically collects a standard set of app-usage data even though we do not currently record custom events. This includes app opens, screen views, session length, device model, operating system version, language, an approximate location derived from your IP address (country/region level, not precise location), and an advertising identifier.

Firebase Crashlytics collects diagnostic data only when the app crashes: stack traces, the device model and OS version, and app state at the moment of the crash. This is used solely to find and fix defects.

Future analytics

If we later introduce custom analytics events to understand which features are used, they will measure app usage only — never the content of your personal communications, and never for advertising. This policy and our Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before any such change ships.

What we never collect

We do not collect precise GPS location. We do not access your contacts, microphone, camera, photos, SMS or call logs. The app requests no such permissions.

3.6 Content delivery

Audio files and artwork are served from Cloudflare R2. When your device requests a track or an image, Cloudflare receives your IP address and standard request headers, as is unavoidable for any internet content delivery. Cloudflare processes this to deliver content and to protect against abuse. Your account identity is not sent with these requests.

5. Cookies and local storage

The HimRaag Android app does not use cookies. It is a native application with no embedded browser or web view, so no HTTP cookies are ever set or read on your device. This website does not set cookies either — it is static and carries no trackers or analytics.

Instead, the app stores data locally using standard platform mechanisms:

Local storage mechanisms used by the app
MechanismPurpose
Local database (Hive)Preferences, favourites, recently played, download state, cached catalogue
Disk image cacheArtwork, cleared automatically after 30 days
Firestore offline persistenceCached documents for offline browsing
Firebase Auth token storeKeeping you signed in
Firebase Analytics identifiersAn app-instance ID and an advertising identifier
Future web version

If HimRaag ever offers a web player, cookies or equivalent browser storage may become necessary for sign-in and preferences. Should that happen, we will publish a full cookie notice and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before any non-essential cookie is set.

6. Legal bases for processing (UK/EU users)

  • Contract — account data and your library, needed to provide the service you asked for.
  • Legitimate interests — crash reporting and analytics, to keep the app stable and understand broad usage.
  • Consent — where required by local law for analytics identifiers; you may withdraw it by uninstalling the app or contacting us.

7. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Data is processed by these service providers only:

Third-party processors and their roles
ProviderRolePolicy
Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics) Authentication, database, analytics, crash reporting firebase.google.com/support/privacy
Cloudflare (R2) Audio and image delivery cloudflare.com/privacypolicy

We may disclose data if legally required to do so.

8. Data retention

Account data is kept for as long as your account exists. If you request deletion, we remove your account record and associated library data from our database. Crash and analytics records are retained according to Google Firebase’s standard retention periods. Locally stored data (guest preferences, downloads, caches) is removed when you uninstall the app.

9. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of your personal data by writing to sc5271027@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to object to processing, to restrict processing, to withdraw consent, or to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Account deletion

You can have your account and its associated data deleted at any time. The full process, including exactly what is removed and what may remain, is set out on our dedicated page:

How to delete your account

In short: email sc5271027@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and we will process the deletion within 30 days. To remove all locally held data immediately, sign out and uninstall the app.

11. Children’s privacy

HimRaag is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it. Where local law sets a higher age of digital consent, that higher age applies.

12. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS; the app forbids cleartext traffic at the platform level. Access to our database is restricted by server-side security rules that limit each signed-in user to their own records. Administrative access requires a separately granted privileged credential.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify affected users and the relevant authority where the law requires it.

13. International transfers

Our providers (Google, Cloudflare) operate globally, so your data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. These providers maintain their own safeguards for international transfers.

14. Copyright and DMCA

HimRaag respects the rights of artists and copyright owners. All music, artwork and recordings remain the property of their respective rights-holders.

If you believe content in HimRaag infringes your copyright, send a notice to sc5271027@gmail.com including identification of the work, where it appears in the app, proof of your ownership or authority to act, your contact details, and a statement made in good faith. Substantiated claims are removed promptly. Full details are on the contact page.

15. Changes to this policy

We will update the “Last updated” date above when this policy changes and, for material changes, give notice in the app.
