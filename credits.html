Background videos

While a song plays, the HimRaag player can show a short, silent nature video behind it. These loops are cut from the videos credited below.

Drone footage by HikingFex

The video material has been kindly provided by HikingFex.com.

https://www.hikingfex.com/en/videos

30 of the loops are cut from three HikingFex videos on YouTube, used under the usage notice in each video's description:

  • Forest Stock Footage | No Copyright Drone Shots (15:47): 17 loops
  • No Copyright Drone Shots | Drone footage nature shots (6:08): 7 loops
  • No Copyright Drone Shots | Drone footage nature shots (3:05): 6 loops

Each loop is a single shot from these videos, cropped to fit a phone screen and made to repeat smoothly.
