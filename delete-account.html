Delete Your Account

Last updated: 6 August 2026  ·  Applies to: HimRaag Android app (com.sumitchauhan.himraag)

You can ask us to permanently delete your HimRaag account and the data associated with it at any time, for any reason. You do not have to give a justification. This page explains exactly how to do it and precisely what happens.

Never signed in? There is nothing to delete.

If you only ever used HimRaag as a guest, none of your data left your device — we hold nothing about you on our servers. Uninstalling the app removes everything. See removing local data.

1. How to request deletion

Account deletion is currently handled by email. We verify the request, delete your records, and confirm in writing when it is done.

  1. Email us from your account address Send a message to sc5271027@gmail.com from the same email address you used to sign in. This is how we confirm the request is really yours — we will not delete an account on the word of a third party.
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my HimRaag account” It routes your request correctly and avoids delay. Nothing else needs to be in the message — please do not send your password (we never have it and never need it).
  3. We verify and delete We match the sending address to the account, delete the records listed below, and remove your authentication record so the account can no longer sign in.
  4. You get written confirmation We reply to confirm the deletion is complete. If we cannot match your address to an account, we tell you that instead — we never silently ignore a request.

Request account deletion

2. What is deleted

When your request is processed, the following are permanently removed from our systems (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore):

Your account record — email, display name, profile photo URL, user IDFirebase Authentication

Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered. If you sign in again later you start with a completely new, empty account.

3. What may remain, and for how long

We want to be straightforward about this rather than promise more than we can deliver:

Data that may persist after account deletion
WhatWhyHow long
Crash reports (Crashlytics) Already aggregated for diagnostics and not linked to your profile in a way we can search by email Google Firebase’s standard retention period, then automatically removed
Analytics records Aggregated, pseudonymous app-usage statistics tied to an app-instance identifier rather than your identity Google Firebase’s standard retention period
Server and CDN access logs (Cloudflare R2) Generated automatically when content is delivered; used for security and abuse prevention Short-term, per the provider’s log retention
Email correspondence with us Record of your request and our response Kept only as long as needed to evidence that we acted on it
Backups Deleted records may persist briefly in routine system backups before those rotate out Cleared on the normal backup rotation
Anything the law requires us to keep Legal or regulatory obligation, or defence of a legal claim Only as long as legally required

None of the above allows us to reconstruct your account, your library or your listening history after deletion.

4. How long it takes

Processed within 30 days — usually much sooner

We aim to acknowledge your request within 2–3 business days and to complete deletion well inside the 30-day commitment in our Privacy Policy. Most requests are done within a few days.

5. Removing data held on your device

Deleting your account clears our servers. Data held locally on your phone is removed separately — and immediately — by you:

  • Downloaded songs — delete them in the app, or uninstall HimRaag to remove them all at once.
  • Guest preferences, favourites and history — stored only on the device; uninstalling removes them.
  • Cached artwork and offline data — removed on uninstall.

Signing out and uninstalling the app deletes every piece of HimRaag data on your device. It does not by itself delete your server-side account — for that, send the email above.

6. Revoking Google Sign-In access

Separately from deleting your HimRaag account, you can revoke the app’s access to your Google account at any time from your Google account’s third-party apps and services page. Doing this stops future sign-ins but does not, on its own, delete the data we already hold — please email us as well.

7. In-app deletion (coming in a future release)

We are building “Delete account” directly into the app, so you will be able to remove your account from the profile screen without emailing anyone. It will ask you to confirm, explain what is about to be deleted, and complete immediately.

Until that ships, the email route on this page is the official method and is honoured in full. This page will be updated when the in-app option is available.
