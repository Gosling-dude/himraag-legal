Delete Your Account

Last updated: 20 October 2026  ·  Applies to: HimRaag Android app (com.sumitchauhan.himraag)

You can ask us to permanently delete your HimRaag account and the data associated with it at any time, for any reason. You do not have to give a justification. This page explains exactly how to do it and precisely what happens.

Never signed in? There is no account to delete.

HimRaag requires a Google sign-in, so if you installed it but never signed in we hold no account data about you. Only the pseudonymous analytics and crash data described in section 3 may exist. Uninstalling the app removes everything on your phone. See removing local data.

1. How to delete your account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Profile → Delete account The app explains what will be deleted and asks you to confirm.
  2. Confirm with Google if asked For security, Firebase only allows deletion shortly after a sign-in, so the app may ask you to choose your Google account again.
  3. Done Your account and your song requests are deleted straight away, and your favourites and listening history on that phone are cleared.

By email

If you no longer have the app, you can ask us by email instead. We verify the request, delete your records, and confirm in writing when it is done.

  1. Email us from your account address Send a message to sc5271027@gmail.com from the same email address you used to sign in. This is how we confirm the request is really yours — we will not delete an account on the word of a third party.
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my HimRaag account” It routes your request correctly and avoids delay. Nothing else needs to be in the message — please do not send your password (we never have it and never need it).
  3. We verify and delete We match the sending address to the account, delete the records listed below, and remove your authentication record so the account can no longer sign in.
  4. You get written confirmation We reply to confirm the deletion is complete. If we cannot match your address to an account, we tell you that instead — we never silently ignore a request.

Request account deletion

2. What is deleted

When your account is deleted (in the app, or by us after an email request), the following are permanently removed:

Data permanently deleted on request
DataWhere it lived
Your account record — email, display name, profile photo URL, user IDFirebase Authentication
Sign-in credentials and session tokensFirebase Authentication
Your song requests — song details, search text, your name and emailCloud Firestore
Favourites and listening history (cleared by in-app deletion on the phone you use)Your phone

Your favourites, listening history, settings and download records are never stored on our servers; they live only on your phone (see section 5).

Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered. If you sign in again later you start with a completely new, empty account.

3. What may remain, and for how long

We want to be straightforward about this rather than promise more than we can deliver:

Data that may persist after account deletion
WhatWhyHow long
Crash reports (Crashlytics) Already aggregated for diagnostics and not linked to your profile in a way we can search by email Google Firebase’s standard retention period, then automatically removed
Analytics records Aggregated, pseudonymous app-usage statistics tied to an app-instance identifier rather than your identity Google Firebase’s standard retention period
Server and CDN access logs (Cloudflare) Generated automatically when content is delivered; used for security and abuse prevention Short-term, per the provider’s log retention
Copies of your song-request alerts Delivered to the developer’s own Telegram chat and mailbox when you sent the request Until the developer deletes them — ask us by email and we will delete them
Daily play counter (only where server-side play counting is active) A single number per account per day, used to limit abuse of play counts Deleted on email request
Email correspondence with us Record of your request and our response Kept only as long as needed to evidence that we acted on it
Backups Deleted records may persist briefly in routine system backups before those rotate out Cleared on the normal backup rotation
Anything the law requires us to keep Legal or regulatory obligation, or defence of a legal claim Only as long as legally required

None of the above allows us to reconstruct your account, your library or your listening history after deletion.

4. How long it takes

In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests: within 30 days — usually much sooner

For email requests, we aim to acknowledge within 2–3 business days and to complete deletion well inside the 30-day commitment in our Privacy Policy. Most requests are done within a few days.

5. Removing data held on your device

Deleting your account clears our servers. Data held locally on your phone is removed separately — and immediately — by you:

  • Downloaded songs — in-app account deletion does not remove them; delete them in the app, or uninstall HimRaag to remove them all at once.
  • Favourites and listening history — stored only on the device; in-app account deletion clears them, and uninstalling removes them.
  • Settings and play counts waiting to be sent — stored only on the device; removed on uninstall.
  • Cached artwork and offline data — removed on uninstall.

Uninstalling the app deletes every piece of HimRaag data on your device. It does not by itself delete your server-side account — for that, use Profile → Delete account or send the email above.

6. Revoking Google Sign-In access

Separately from deleting your HimRaag account, you can revoke the app’s access to your Google account at any time from your Google account’s third-party apps and services page. Doing this stops future sign-ins but does not, on its own, delete the data we already hold — please also delete your account in the app or email us.

7. In-app deletion

“Delete account” is built into the app under Profile. It asks you to confirm, explains what is about to be deleted, and completes immediately. The email route above remains available and is honoured in full.
